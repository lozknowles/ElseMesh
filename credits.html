Loz's Warehouse Island

Return to the island

This download contains the ElseMesh world engine, a pinned public copy of Loz's authored island, and two stock player characters. The island includes reusable warehouse furniture prepared from the ElseMesh warehouse recipe. Its own repository and this release retain attribution, provenance, and asset licences.
