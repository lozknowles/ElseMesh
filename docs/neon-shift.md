# Neon Shift

The violet third Warehouse doorway leads to an original procedural neon city. Its surrounding skyline changes position, size, silhouette and colour every 60 seconds, cycling Rain City, Circuit Arena and Arcade Sky compositions. A fixed 48 m plaza, flat floor and permanent Warehouse return gate provide a safe walking area. Changing towers stay beyond 28 m from the plaza centre on at least one horizontal axis throughout their morph; the authored collision boundary has inner faces at ±24 m and is 10 m high. The visual forcefield is transparent, while collision remains solid. Floating art and traffic stay above player jump height. No film assets, remote art, downloaded scripts or generated collision replacements are used.

The engine-owned adapter is admitted only for world `elsemesh.world:81ae2994-59ba-499b-bb79-ba72927f6720` and manifest `a914aa4ba9a8efe7e0b4afcc5115be414fe00a93728d28b4998ad32929264838`. Source revision `8d2e138c783a8cbbee3212eb4060839e807ee0f0` sets 21:00 and rain. Original scene and procedural art use the application's MIT licence. The public qualification is WebGL; importing its data into another renderer does not establish equivalent procedural visuals.

One wall-clock minute state is computed per animation frame and shared by preview and active scenes. Entry never restarts the timer. A forward clock jump goes directly to the current generation; rollback freezes at the latest observed time until wall time catches up. A shift interpolates for 2.4 seconds inside fixed outer plot zones. Rain, traffic, rings and suspended wire sculptures animate continuously. The countdown uses one reused canvas texture and updates once per second.

The fixed procedural pool contains 80 towers, 1,914 instances, 6 geometries, 7 materials and 2 textures. It has 23 mesh objects and 26,974 triangles before renderer-specific passes. Shifts update transforms, colours and existing texture pixels; they do not allocate new GPU resource identities or change shader structure. Existing preparation leases and disposal own the entire candidate. Up to three public previews now coexist; normal owner/network and native preview limits stay at two. Existing distance/frustum culling remains active. Extra previews have a rendering cost; these bounds are not an FPS guarantee or a whole-game memory-stability claim.
