Tips
  • WASD to walk, Space to jump and Shift to run.
  • Drag the mouse or use arrow keys to look. Press V to switch between first-person and follow view.
  • Press E near a computer. Access restricted means the world owner must grant you permission.
  • Move around a live portal to see the destination from different angles. Walk through when it is ready.
  • Viewing a world, entering it and using its objects have separate permissions.
  • Owners manage visitor permissions for individual objects. Your access may differ in each world.
  • If a destination is offline or restricted, check its portal status and remain in your current world.
